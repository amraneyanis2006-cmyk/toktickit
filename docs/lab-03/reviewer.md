## Summary

Across the Lab 3 sprint, Simeon's review comments consistently targeted the same two things: **whether a stated design decision was actually load-bearing** (the Administrator ticket-power call in #48, the client-side status matrix in #52) and **whether a test claiming to prove something actually proved it** (the 8h session-expiry simulation in #48, the priority-filter fixture weakness in #51, the missing Reassign concurrency test in #52). The priority-filter issue (#51) was fixed before merge, on the spot. Two issues were consciously merged with the gap stated rather than fixed in-PR — the Reassign test (#52) and the password-delivery UX (#53) — and both were resolved during the Issue #21 integration pass rather than left open at submission. The #53 exchange also surfaced something neither of us caught in review: the original "log the password to the server console" implementation was not just a UX gap but a direct violation of BR-04 (passwords must never be logged in plaintext) already stated in `specification.md` — found only later, during a dedicated Definition-of-Done traceability pass in #21, by re-reading the spec's own business rules against the shipped code rather than trusting that a prior review had already caught it.
